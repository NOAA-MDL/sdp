**DISCLAIMER.md**

### 1. Emergency Management Authority
Pay attention to your local emergency manager, particularly during an
evacuation. **DO NOT use the SLOSH Display program as an excuse to ignore your
local emergency manager!**

The SLOSH Display program is only one of several tools used by emergency
management agencies to determine who is at risk and may be asked to evacuate.

### 2. Recommended Training
We recommend people have training before a hurricane impacts their lives. The
key ideas to learn are:
* What is storm surge?
* There is inaccuracy in any model.
* There is more inaccuracy in the input wind parameters to surge models than in
  the models themselves.
